# Blood Conditions and CBC Abnormalities

## Anemia

Anemia is a condition in which the blood has a lower-than-expected amount of healthy red blood cells or hemoglobin. It can reduce the blood's ability to carry oxygen.

## Polycythemia

Polycythemia refers to an increased concentration of red blood cells. The finding can have several causes and requires appropriate clinical evaluation.

## Leukocytosis

Leukocytosis refers to an elevated white blood cell count. It can occur in infections, inflammation, stress responses, medication effects, and certain blood disorders.

## Leukopenia

Leukopenia refers to a lower-than-expected white blood cell count. Possible causes include infections, medications, autoimmune conditions, and bone marrow disorders.

## Thrombocytopenia

Thrombocytopenia means a lower-than-expected platelet count. It may be associated with increased bleeding risk, depending on the severity and underlying cause.

## Thrombocytosis

Thrombocytosis refers to an elevated platelet count. It may occur as a response to infection or inflammation, or due to a bone marrow condition.

## Neutropenia

Neutropenia refers to a lower-than-expected neutrophil count. The significance depends on the absolute neutrophil count and the clinical context.

## Neutrophilia

Neutrophilia refers to an elevated neutrophil count. It can occur during infections, inflammation, stress responses, and certain other conditions.

## Lymphocytopenia

Lymphocytopenia refers to a lower-than-expected lymphocyte count. It may be associated with infections, immune conditions, medications, or other medical factors.

## Lymphocytosis

Lymphocytosis refers to an elevated lymphocyte count. It can occur in some infections and other immune or blood-related conditions.

## Monocytes High

An elevated monocyte count is called monocytosis. It can be associated with infections, inflammatory conditions, and certain blood disorders.

## Eosinophil High

An elevated eosinophil count is called eosinophilia. It can occur in allergic conditions, parasitic infections, medication reactions, and other conditions.

## Basophil High

An elevated basophil count is called basophilia. It may occur in allergic or inflammatory conditions and certain blood disorders.

## Normal

A normal CBC classification means the model assigned the sample to its normal class. It does not guarantee that a person is free of disease. Results must still be interpreted in clinical context.

## Important Disclaimer

These descriptions are educational summaries, not diagnostic criteria. An abnormal CBC value does not independently establish a disease. A qualified healthcare professional should interpret laboratory results.
